Reveal-Aware Wiki Generation for Fictional Series

In Progress

Current independent research. Investigates Temporal Knowledge Graphs (TKG) and GraphRAG to automatically build wikis for fictional series that stay spoiler-aware: a reader browsing up to a given point in the story never sees information revealed later. The system maintains a densely connected graph of characters, events, and plot threads while anchoring every generated claim to its source passage, aiming to keep the knowledge base resistant to contradiction and hallucination as new content is added.

  • Temporal Knowledge Graphs
  • GraphRAG
  • Spoiler-Aware NLP
  • Reference-Grounded Generation
  • Knowledge Base Construction

Benchmarking Psychotherapy Datasets Using RAG and LoRA

Dec 2024 — Sept 2025

Undergraduate thesis. Explores Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and Low-Rank Adaptation (LoRA) to improve LLM-based dialogue generation for psychological therapy. Uses LLM-as-a-judge to evaluate response quality across multiple therapy datasets.
