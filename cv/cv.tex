\cvsection{Research Experience}

\cvitem{\href{https://zunaid13.github.io/Portfolio/assets/narrativewiki-paper.pdf}{NarrativeWiki}}{Under Review $\cdot$ 2026}
\cvsub{``NarrativeWiki: Probing the Trade-offs of Decomposition in Graph-Built Character Wikis at Reader-Selected Narrative \mbox{Cutoffs}''}
\cvsub{\textbf{Md. Zunaid-Ul-Alam}, Syed Rifat Raiyan $\cdot$ \href{https://zunaid13.github.io/Portfolio/assets/narrativewiki-paper.pdf}{Paper (PDF)} $\cdot$ \href{https://github.com/zunaid13/narrativewiki}{Code}}
\begin{cvitemize}
  \item Built NarrativeWiki, which decomposes a novel series into a graph of evidence-backed claims dated by volume and filters it at the reader's chosen cutoff before generating a spoiler-safe, linked character wiki.
  \item Evaluated it against six systems at every cutoff of a five-volume series (111 gold facts), with a renamed-and-paraphrased text control: closed-book recall fell from 0.683 to 0.003, while systems given the text lost at most 0.070.
  \item Showed that the design trades context for accumulation: it best covers the most-documented characters and loses one fact across cutoffs (other systems: 4--24), but stored claims lose speaker, modality and scene context.
\end{cvitemize}
